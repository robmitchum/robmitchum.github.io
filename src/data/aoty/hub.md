## Intro
Album of the year lists from music publications, combined into one list.

## What this is
My yearly effort to collate the album of the year lists from music publications into a single spreadsheet. It started as a good way to catch albums I missed over the past year, and to have all the lists in one place for easy browsing. One could also use it to make some arguments semi-supported by numbers about music criticism and trends and so forth, if one wished.

## Which lists count
I only choose lists that meet the following criteria:

- A list voted on by a staff, not a single person.
- A list that covers multiple genres, not just metal, rap, etc.
- At least 50 albums are listed (though I only take the top 50).

## How albums are ranked
I list albums 1-50 according to their rank on each list. So low numbers are good, like golf. The "Consensus Score" takes the average ranking across all lists and penalizes an album for every list it doesn't appear on, by assigning a pseudo-rank of 75.

## Can I help?
At the most basic level, if you notice a mistake, shout it out...maintaining the list is a lot of typing. If you know some fancy statistics or visualizations we can run on the data, I'd love to hear it. If you want to play with the numbers yourself and can't get them off of Google Sheets for some reason, let me know. Please contact me on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/robmitchum.bsky.social) or rdmitchum at gmail. Thanks!
