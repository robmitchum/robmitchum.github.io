Rob Mitchum is a music and science writer and editor in Oak Park, Illinois. He is currently the editor in chief of [Kellogg Insight](https://insight.kellogg.northwestern.edu/), the digital magazine for faculty research and ideas at the Kellog School of Management at Northwestern University.

Rob has previously worked in science and research communications for the University of Illinois Chicago and the University of Chicago. At UChicago, he led communications for the Data Science Institute, the Department of Computer Science, and the Computation Institute, and served as senior science writer with UChicago Medicine. He has a PhD in neurobiology from the University of Chicago, and spent two years as a reporter at the Chicago Tribune, covering science as well as general news.

Rob's music writing has appeared at Pitchfork, Uncut, Paste, Spin, MTV, Deadspin, and many other publications. His primary current writing project is [Phishcrit](https://phishcrit.substack.com/), publishing essays on every Phish show 25 years after it happened, and he is the co-host of [36FTVQ](https://stevenhyden.substack.com/t/36ftvq) with Steven Hyden, a podcast on the live releases of the Grateful Dead. Despite what you may have heard, he didn't come up with the phrase "dad-rock"...but he gets [asked about it a lot](https://www.esquire.com/entertainment/music/a29419783/what-is-dad-rock/).

You can reach Rob at [contact@robmitchum.com](mailto:contact@robmitchum.com) or on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/robmitchum.bsky.social).
